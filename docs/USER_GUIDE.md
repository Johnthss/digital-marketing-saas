# User Guide

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Navigate to your agency's Digital Marketing SaaS URL
2. Click **"Sign Up"** on the login page
3. Enter your name, email, and a strong password
4. Provide your agency name — this becomes your workspace
5. Verify your email address (if email verification is enabled)
6. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Logging In

1. Go to `https://your-domain.com/login`
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. If you have access to multiple agencies, select the one you want to work with

### Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a real-time overview of your marketing operations:

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Quick Stats** | Active campaigns, scheduled posts, pending tasks, unread messages |
| **Performance Chart** | Engagement metrics over time (likes, shares, clicks, impressions) |
| **Recent Activity** | Latest actions by team members |
| **Upcoming Posts** | Scheduled content for the next 7 days |
| **AI Suggestions** | Recommended actions from your AI assistant |
| **Task List** | Pending approvals, reviews, and assignments |

### Navigation

The left sidebar provides access to all features:

- **Dashboard** — Overview and analytics
- **Campaigns** — Marketing campaign management
- **Social Media** — Posts, scheduling, and accounts
- **Email Marketing** — Email campaigns and templates
- **Content Library** — Saved content and templates
- **AI Assistant** — AI-powered content and agents
- **Workflows** — Automation builder
- **Analytics** — Reports and insights
- **Clients** — Client management (CRM)
- **Invoices** — Billing and invoicing
- **Media Library** — File management
- **Team** — Team member management
- **Settings** — Agency configuration

## Creating Campaigns

### Campaign Types

- **Social Media Campaigns** — Multi-post campaigns across platforms
- **Email Campaigns** — Targeted email sends to client lists
- **Landing Pages** — Standalone pages for lead capture

### Creating a Social Media Campaign

1. Navigate to **Campaigns** → **All Campaigns**
2. Click **"New Campaign"**
3. Fill in the campaign details:
   - **Name** — Internal campaign name
   - **Description** — Campaign objectives and notes
   - **Client** — Select the associated client
   - **Platforms** — Choose which social platforms to use
   - **Budget** — Optional budget allocation
   - **Date Range** — Campaign start and end dates
4. Click **"Create Campaign"**
5. Add posts to the campaign:
   - Click **"Add Post"**
   - Write your content or use AI to generate it
   - Select platforms and accounts
   - Choose to publish now or schedule
   - Add media (images, videos)
6. Review and approve the campaign

### Creating an Email Campaign

1. Navigate to **Email Marketing** → **Campaigns**
2. Click **"New Email Campaign"**
3. Configure the campaign:
   - **Name** — Internal campaign name
   - **Subject Line** — Email subject (use personalization variables)
   - **From Name** — Sender name
   - **Reply-To** — Reply-to email address
   - **Recipient List** — Select a client or segment
4. Design your email:
   - Choose a template or start from scratch
   - Use the drag-and-drop editor
   - Add images, buttons, and dynamic content
   - Preview on desktop and mobile
5. Send a test email to yourself
6. Choose to send immediately or schedule

### Campaign Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Draft** | Campaign is being created, not yet active |
| **Active** | Campaign is running and publishing content |
| **Paused** | Campaign temporarily stopped |
| **Completed** | Campaign has ended |
| **Archived** | Campaign stored for reference |

## Managing Social Accounts

### Connecting Social Media Accounts

1. Navigate to **Social Media** → **Accounts**
2. Click **"Connect Account"**
3. Select the platform:
   - **Twitter/X** — Authorize via OAuth
   - **Facebook** — Connect via Facebook Login
   - **Instagram** — Connect via Facebook (Meta Business)
   - **LinkedIn** — Authorize via OAuth
   - **TikTok** — Authorize via TikTok Login Kit
4. Grant the requested permissions
5. The account appears in your connected accounts list

### Scheduling Posts

1. Navigate to **Social Media** → **Posts**
2. Click **"New Post"**
3. Select the accounts to publish to
4. Write your content:
   - Use the text editor with character count
   - Add hashtags and mentions
   - Attach images or videos
5. Choose when to publish:
   - **Publish Now** — Immediately
   - **Schedule** — Set a specific date and time
   - **Best Time** — Let AI choose the optimal time
6. Click **"Schedule"** or **"Publish"**

### Content Calendar

The content calendar provides a visual overview of all scheduled posts:

- **Month View** — See all posts for the month
- **Week View** — Detailed weekly schedule
- **Day View** — Hour-by-hour breakdown
- **Drag & Drop** — Reschedule posts by dragging

### Platform Analytics

View performance metrics for each connected account:

- **Followers** — Growth over time
- **Engagement** — Likes, comments, shares, clicks
- **Reach** — Impressions and unique viewers
- **Top Posts** — Best performing content
- **Audience** — Demographics and activity patterns

## Using AI Agents

### AI Content Generation

1. Navigate to **AI Assistant** → **Generate Content**
2. Select the content type:
   - **Social Post** — Captions and tweets
   - **Email Copy** — Subject lines and body text
   - **Blog Post** — Long-form articles
   - **Ad Copy** — Advertisement text
   - **Hashtags** — Relevant hashtag suggestions
3. Provide context:
   - Topic or product description
   - Target audience
   - Tone of voice (professional, casual, humorous)
   - Desired length
4. Click **"Generate"**
5. Review and edit the suggestions
6. Save to content library or use directly

### AI Agents

AI agents are autonomous assistants that perform complex tasks:

| Agent | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Lead Generation** | Identifies and qualifies potential clients |
| **Social Strategy** | Analyzes performance and recommends strategy |
| **Weekly Report** | Generates comprehensive performance reports |
| **Security Audit** | Scans for security vulnerabilities |
| **Content Optimizer** | Improves existing content for engagement |

### Running an Agent

1. Navigate to **AI Assistant** → **Agents**
2. Select the agent you want to run
3. Configure the parameters:
   - Date range
   - Platforms to analyze
   - Specific campaigns or clients
4. Click **"Run Agent"**
5. Wait for the agent to complete (usually 1-5 minutes)
6. Review the results and recommendations

### AI Assistant via Telegram

Connect with your AI assistant on the go:

1. Open Telegram and search for your bot username
2. Send `/start` to begin
3. Link your account with `/link`
4. Use natural language commands:
   - "Show me this week's performance"
   - "Generate a tweet about our new product"
   - "What campaigns are active?"
   - "Create a report for client X"

### AI Recommendations

The AI assistant provides proactive suggestions:

- **Content Ideas** — Based on trending topics and your niche
- **Optimal Posting Times** — Based on audience activity
- **Hashtag Suggestions** — Relevant and trending hashtags
- **A/B Test Ideas** — Variations to test for better engagement
- **Budget Allocation** — Recommendations for ad spend distribution

## Generating Reports

### Report Types

| Report | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Performance Overview** | High-level metrics across all channels |
| **Campaign Report** | Detailed analysis of a specific campaign |
| **Social Media Report** | Platform-specific engagement metrics |
| **Email Report** | Open rates, click rates, conversions |
| **Client Report** | Client-specific performance summary |
| **Custom Report** | Build your own with selected metrics |

### Creating a Report

1. Navigate to **Analytics** → **Reports**
2. Click **"New Report"**
3. Select the report type
4. Configure the parameters:
   - **Date Range** — Time period to analyze
   - **Platforms** — Which social accounts to include
   - **Campaigns** — Filter by specific campaigns
   - **Metrics** — Select which metrics to display
5. Click **"Generate Report"**
6. View the interactive report
7. Export or share:
   - **PDF** — For presentations and clients
   - **CSV** — For data analysis
   - **Excel** — For detailed spreadsheets
   - **Share Link** — Send a view-only link

### Scheduled Reports

Automate report delivery:

1. Create a report as described above
2. Click **"Schedule"**
3. Set the frequency:
   - Daily
   - Weekly (choose day and time)
   - Monthly (choose date and time)
4. Add recipients (team members or client emails)
5. Choose the format (PDF recommended)
6. Click **"Save Schedule"**

### Report Metrics

Available metrics vary by report type:

**Social Media Metrics:**
- Impressions and reach
- Engagement rate
- Likes, comments, shares, saves
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Follower growth
- Top performing posts
- Audience demographics

**Email Metrics:**
- Delivery rate
- Open rate
- Click rate
- Bounce rate
- Unsubscribe rate
- Conversion rate
- Revenue per email

**Campaign Metrics:**
- Total spend
- Cost per click (CPC)
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Conversion count
- Impression share

### Managing Your Subscription

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. View your current plan and usage
3. **Upgrade/Downgrade:**
   - Click **"Change Plan"**
   - Select the new plan
   - Confirm the change (prorated billing applies)
4. **Update Payment Method:**
   - Click **"Update Payment"**
   - Enter new card details
   - Stripe handles secure processing
5. **View Invoices:**
   - Click **"Billing History"**
   - Download PDF invoices
   - See payment status

### Creating Invoices (for Clients)

Agency owners can create invoices for their clients:

1. Navigate to **Invoices** → **All Invoices**
2. Click **"New Invoice"**
3. Select the client
4. Add line items:
   - Description
   - Quantity
   - Unit price
   - Tax rate (if applicable)
5. Set the due date
6. Add notes or payment terms
7. Click **"Save Invoice"**
8. **Send to Client:**
   - Click **"Send"** to email the invoice
   - Client receives a link to view and pay online
   - Stripe handles payment processing

### Invoice Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Draft** | Invoice created but not sent |
| **Sent** | Invoice emailed to client |
| **Viewed** | Client has opened the invoice |
| **Paid** | Payment received |
| **Overdue** | Past due date, payment not received |
| **Cancelled** | Invoice voided |

### Stripe Integration

All payments are processed securely through Stripe:

- **Credit/Debit Cards** — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover
- **Digital Wallets** — Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **Automatic Invoicing** — Recurring subscription billing
- **Webhook Handling** — Real-time payment status updates
- **PCI Compliance** — Stripe handles all card data securely

### Refunds and Credits

1. Navigate to the invoice
2. Click **"Refund"**
3. Choose full or partial refund
4. Add a reason (optional)
5. Confirm the refund
6. The client receives the refund to their original payment method
