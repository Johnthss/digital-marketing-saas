## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- **Vendor lock-in**: Multi-cloud strategy, open standards
- **Scalability**: Horizontal scaling, caching, CDN
- **Security**: Regular audits, bug bounty program
- **Technical debt**: 20% time for refactoring

### Business Risks
- **Competition**: Focus on UX, customer success
- **Market changes**: Agile development, customer feedback
- **Regulatory**: Proactive compliance, legal team
- **Economic**: Diversified revenue, cost optimization

### Operational Risks
- **Key person risk**: Documentation, cross-training
- **Burnout**: Work-life balance, mental health support
- **Communication**: Async-first, documentation culture
- **Quality**: Automated testing, code reviews
